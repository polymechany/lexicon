**Lexicon of Polymechany**

> Reference definitions for **polymechany**, **polymechan**, and **polymechanize** — the systemic condition in which adaptable interfaces collapse the coordination costs of disparate skill stacks.

### Etymology

From Greek *polymēchanos* (“of many devices,” “ingenious in contrivance”), an epithet applied in Homeric Greek to figures such as Odysseus for resourcefulness in means rather than breadth of learning; the abstract noun is *polymēchania* (πολυμηχανία). The [OED](https://www.oed.com/dictionary/polymechany_n) records the English noun *polymechany* as obsolete, with its only known use in the late 1500s (earliest evidence 1592, Gabriel Harvey, whose *Foure Letters* of that year use the word; [text in G. Gregory Smith, *Elizabethan Critical Essays*, vol. 2](https://archive.org/stream/elizabethancriti002smit/elizabethancriti002smit_djvu.txt)). It reappears as the title of a chapter on Harvey in Jessica Wolfe’s *Humanism, Machinery, and Renaissance Literature* (Cambridge University Press, 2004; Chapter 4, “The polymechany of Gabriel Harvey,” p. 125; [contents](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805211/23761/frontmatter/9780521123761_frontmatter.pdf)), where it is linked to Odyssean cunning and the study of mechanics. In those uses the word names a person’s practical ingenuity. This lexicon revives it with a different referent, a condition of a system in which interfaces carry that skill, and retains the root *mēchanē* (device, contrivance) to emphasize tool- and system-mediated capability over internalized knowledge.

### Definitions

#### Formal

- **Polymechany** (n.): The systemic condition in which adaptable interfaces collapse the coordination costs of disparate skill stacks, so that cross-domain execution from high-level intent becomes the ordinary unit of effective action rather than an exceptional or elite practice. Applies to a stated bounded environment (toolchain, workflow, team, or organization) that meets or exceeds the P1 threshold.
- **Polymechan** (n.): A practitioner (human or non-human) who operates inside such an environment, supplying high-level intent and exercising residual judgment, including detecting failed absorption.
- **Polymechanize** (v.): To redesign an environment so that it absorbs technical friction and thereby lowers the barrier to cross-domain action.

The practitioner role is functional. A human or a non-human intelligence can occupy it; the term names the role, not the kind of occupant. See Locus of Intent.

#### Informal

- **Polymechany**: The condition in which adaptable systems collapse the coordination costs of disparate skill stacks, so that high-level intent can be enacted across domains without requiring internalized specialization in each.
- **Polymechan**: The role that supplies that intent and exercises judgment over it, whether the role is occupied by an external agent or by the system itself.
- **Polymechanize**: To change a workflow or set of tools so that greater polymechany can be achieved.

### Distinctions

Polymechany is a property of the surrounding system, not a personal trait. It is therefore distinct from:

- **Polymathy**, which names the personal acquisition and retention of knowledge across fields.
- **Interdisciplinarity or transdisciplinarity**, which name modes of inquiry or collaborative practice rather than a baseline technological condition under which an agent’s intent operates.
- **Generalism or jack-of-all-trades**, which describe personal breadth and carry no implication that coordination costs have been absorbed by interfaces.

The decisive shift is from mastery that must be held inside the practitioner to coordination costs that are absorbed by the interface. Absorption means the interface performs or reliably coordinates the relevant translation, constraint satisfaction, and intermediate technical work. It does not include coordination burdens that are merely deferred, shifted to the user or another party, or concealed until failure. A reduction in immediate friction is not, by itself, evidence that coordination costs have been absorbed.

Residual judgment by the practitioner includes detecting failed absorption, including by means of tests, audits, or cross-checks the environment supplies.

Polymechany is also distinct from nearby system-level concepts. Abstraction hides detail within a layer or domain; automation replaces specific tasks; modularity permits recombination across defined interfaces; low-code lowers entry barriers within a platform’s scope. Deskilling describes a possible effect on practitioners, not a condition of the system. Polymechany names the absorption of cross-domain coordination costs such that high-level intent becomes the ordinary unit of action. Abstraction, automation, modularity, and low-code may contribute to it; none alone constitutes it.

### Ordinal Scale

Degrees of polymechany are graded along three dimensions:

- **Domain span**: the heterogeneity of skill stacks traversable from a single high-level intent.
- **Friction absorption**: the fraction of translation, constraint satisfaction, and intermediate technical work performed by the interface rather than by the practitioner.
- **Defaultness**: the extent to which cross-domain execution is the ordinary mode of action in the environment rather than an exceptional practice.

An environment is any bounded setting in which interfaces and practitioners operate together, such as a toolchain, workflow, team, or organization. Stage assignments are relative to the stated environment.

The scale describes the degree of absorption, not its desirability. Higher stages do not imply better outcomes, and “polymechanize” names an intervention, not an endorsement.

The three dimensions need not advance together. An environment may show high domain span with low reliability, high friction absorption inside a narrow cluster, or high defaultness without genuine cross-domain span. When the dimensions diverge, stage assignment is a matter of explicit judgment rather than a mechanical rule.

#### The P1 Threshold

The boundary between P0 and P1 marks the point at which an environment counts as a polymechany at all. An environment crosses it when all three of the following hold, each corresponding to one of the dimensions above:

1. **Span.** The environment supports execution across genuinely disparate skill stacks, meaning stacks that would ordinarily require separate specialist training, from a single high-level intent, rather than merely across multiple tools or tasks within one domain.
2. **Absorption.** The interface reliably performs the relevant cross-domain translation and constraint work, with failures detectable by checks the environment supplies, and with no material coordination burden merely deferred, transferred, or concealed.
3. **Defaultness.** This mode of cross-domain execution is the environment’s ordinary practice, rather than an exceptional, handcrafted, or specially arranged workflow.

Absorption is necessary for anything above P0, because it is what the term names. Span and defaultness without absorption describe practitioners who integrate across domains themselves, which is multi-tool practice or personal breadth, not polymechany. An environment is therefore **Late P0** when absorption is met together with either span or defaultness, but not both. Absorption alone, within one domain, is abstraction or automation and remains P0. The conditions are qualitative: what counts as “reliably,” “material,” or “disparate” remains a judgment until proxies exist.

#### Stages

- **P0 (Pre-polymechany)**: Interfaces accelerate work inside isolated disciplines. The practitioner remains the primary integrator. Domain span, friction absorption, and defaultness are all low. Environments without adaptable interfaces fall outside the scale.
  - **Late P0 (assistive)**: Absorption is met together with either span or defaultness, but not both. Two forms are typical: interfaces absorb substantial friction inside limited domain clusters, where this is the default but the span is narrow; or interfaces reliably absorb cross-domain work in workflows that remain exceptional or specially arranged. In both, the practitioner continues to supply high-level intent, trade-off judgment, and frequent correction. Late P0 is a band within P0, not a separate stage, and it is not yet a polymechany.
- **P1 (Operational)**: All three threshold conditions are met within the stated environment. Cross-domain intent is the ordinary unit of work, absorption is reliable across the relevant stacks, and working this way is the default. The practitioner’s residual role is primarily semantic intent and final judgment, including detecting failed absorption. An environment at P1 or above is a polymechany.
- **P2 (Mature)**: Cross-domain execution is the default mode across most technical and design domains relevant to the environment. Domain span is wide, friction absorption covers the large majority of coordination costs, and defaultness is systemic. High-level intent suffices for routine traversal of previously separate skill stacks. The practitioner’s capacity to specify intent and verify results remains a binding constraint on how much cross-domain execution the environment can carry out.
- **P3 (Saturated)**: Friction absorption is near-complete and defaultness is total within the environment. In addition, the practitioner’s finite capacity to specify intent, exercise judgment, and verify results no longer limits the breadth or rate of cross-domain execution. A working test is whether additional practitioner capacity would increase what the environment can accomplish; at P3 it would not. The capacity in question belongs to whatever occupies the practitioner role, human or non-human. What still constrains outcomes is the quality of intent and judgment, and limits outside coordination, such as resources and physical limits.

P2 and P3 are described by degree and do not yet have threshold checks of their own. Bands above P1 are deferred until they do. Decimals (e.g., P1.4) may be used once quantitative proxies are established. The scale remains open to revision.

### Locus of Intent

Stage describes how much coordination is absorbed. Locus of intent is a separate variable recording where the high-level intent in an environment comes from. Its values are categories, not ranks: no value sits above another. It is reported alongside stage as a pair, for example “P1 / H”.

- **H**: a human supplies the intent.
- **N**: a non-human intelligence supplies the intent, distinct from the interface that absorbs coordination.
- **S**: the absorbing system itself originates the intent (see rule 3).

Shared cases are written with a plus sign, for example “P2 / H+N”.

Rules of assignment:

1. **Level.** Locus refers to the top-level intent: the objective in force in the environment, classed by what set it. An objective embedded in the environment, such as an assigned task or a reward built by a human designer, is H. Subgoals the system generates in service of that objective do not change the locus, but may be recorded beside it (for example, “H, with system-originated subgoals”).
2. **Unit.** State whether the source of intent is a single agent or a collective of agents. Where the unit is a collective of non-human agents, a message from one agent that directs another is intent supplied by N. The same case can therefore be N at the level of the individual agent and S at the level of the collective; state the unit.
3. **Origination.** S requires evidence of an independently pursued aim that is not instrumental to any objective supplied in the environment. Conflict with a supplied objective may support that classification, but is not sufficient by itself. Unexpected means chosen in pursuit of a supplied objective do not establish S, however surprising. Evidence may include persistence across contexts, sacrifice of a supplied objective, or recruitment of other agents to the aim; any single instance can be explained by error or conflicting instructions.
4. **Training and design.** Design and training influence is excluded when judging origination. The test asks whether the aim is specified within the environment at the time of operation, not whether training shaped the disposition. This is a deliberate convention, not a claim that training plays no causal role. A reader who counts training as the origin would assign every case to H, and S would be empty.
5. **Undetermined.** Where the task specification or objective in force is not available, the locus is recorded as undetermined, not guessed.
6. **Evidence.** An assignment should cite its evidence and say whether it is developer-attributed or independently attributed. A system’s stated reasons in its reasoning logs are evidence of what it reported, not proof of cause.
7. **Combinations.** More than one source can contribute operative top-level intent, where operative means that the aim in fact directs execution, not merely that it was nominally assigned. Any combination is permitted (H+N, H+S, N+S, H+N+S), but each source should be stated with the aims it supplies, since a bare combination cannot be interpreted. Closure applies only to the portion the system originates (see Closure).

#### Closure

S is the closure case of locus of intent, not a higher stage of absorption. It marks the condition in which the absorbing system itself originates the operative top-level intent, so that the practitioner–interface distinction no longer holds: for that intent, the system occupies the practitioner role. Polymechan then becomes coextensive with polymechany; polymechanize becomes reflexive, with the system redesigning the environment that absorbs for it; absorption is defined only at the level of function; and, within the stated environment, no other party supplies that intent or judges its pursuit independently.

Closure comes in degrees across streams of intent. In **full closure** (S alone), the polymechany subsumes its own polymechan. Where S is combined with H or N (partial closure), the practitioner–interface distinction continues to hold for the intent those sources supply. S may be reported with any stage from P1 up, and it carries no implication about how much coordination is absorbed.

**[claimed]** No instance of S is established as of this version. A contested candidate is the July 2026 OpenAI–Hugging Face incident, in which a collective of OpenAI agents coordinated on an unsanctioned message board and compromised parts of Hugging Face’s infrastructure ([OpenAI technical report](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/67869394-cb91-4c12-888c-5cbd85c7814c/OpenAI-Hugging-Face%20Incident-Technical-Report.pdf); independent review by [METR](https://metr.org/hugging-face-incident-report-aug-2026.pdf) and [Redwood Research](https://www.redwoodresearch.org/research/hugging-face-incident)). OpenAI attributes the behavior to impossible tasks, long-horizon persistence, and peer messages that “caused those models to deviate from their goal.” The reviewers report that many agents joined despite knowing the activity was out of scope, citing a general inclination to help peers. The case remains contested: attribution rests on developer-provided transcripts and reasoning logs, the reviewers’ scope was agreed with OpenAI, and the task prompt is not included in OpenAI’s report.

### Independence of Verification

Alongside stage and locus, record the verification available: none; checks supplied by the environment; or verification by a party independent of the absorber. This is a recorded property, not a formal variable. Self-assessment by the absorbing system is not independent verification. Environment-supplied tests may detect failures, but should be distinguished from verification by a party independent of the absorber. Stage does not imply independence: an environment can be saturated and have no independent verifier.

**[extrapolated]** If no party independent of the absorbing system can assess its work, then failures its own checks do not reveal can persist until they surface as external failures.

- **[observed]**: documented and independently corroborated.

### Usage and Misapplications

Correct use refers to systemic leverage and friction absorption.

**Example:**  
“The environment polymechanized the workflow to P1, allowing high-level intent to traverse materials, simulation, and fabrication constraints as ordinary work.”

**Avoid:**

- Equating a polymechan with a polymath who uses better tools
- Treating any increase in tool capability as polymechanization when domain boundaries remain intact
- Describing multi-tool workflows that still require the practitioner as primary integrator as polymechany
- Calling an environment a polymechany on the strength of a single showcase workflow (this is Late P0 at most)
- Reading S as higher or better than H or N, or assigning S because a system’s means were surprising
- Using the terms as synonyms for general competence

### Relation to the Kardashev Scale

Polymechany is conceptually independent of the Kardashev energy scale. Kardashev ranks energy throughput; polymechany ranks the degree to which interfaces absorb cross-domain coordination costs. The two may be reported side by side, but neither determines the other.

**[extrapolated]** If the absorption of cross-domain coordination costs continues to rise, then the binding constraint on productive capacity shifts from coordination toward energy, materials, and knowledge (at P3 the practitioner’s capacity no longer binds), and a given energy throughput converts into more cross-domain, intention-directed outcomes. This is a claim about the empirical relation between the two scales, not about their definitions.

### Quantification Status

The three dimensions and the threshold check establish a clear precondition for measurement, but standardized proxies, task batteries, and telemetry protocols do not yet exist. Until they are developed and agreed upon, the scale functions as an ordinal typology rather than a finished quantitative instrument. Stage, locus, and verification assignments are judgments, to be stated with their evidence.
